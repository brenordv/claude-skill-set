# Standard ADR Template

```markdown
# ADR-[NNN]: [Decision Title]

## Status
Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-[NNN]

## Context
[What problem are we solving? What are the constraints?]

## Decision Drivers
- [Driver 1, tied to requirement or constraint]
- [Driver 2]

## Considered Options

### Option 1: [Name]
- **Pros**: [Benefits]
- **Cons**: [Drawbacks]

### Option 2: [Name]
- **Pros**: [Benefits]
- **Cons**: [Drawbacks]

## Decision
We will use [Option X].

## Rationale
[Why this option best satisfies the drivers and constraints]

## Consequences
### Positive
- [Benefits gained]

### Negative
- [Costs accepted]

### Risks and Mitigations
- Risk: [description] / Mitigation: [strategy]
```
