# Email sequence

Write a 5-7 email onboarding or re-engagement sequence, ready to load into a sending tool. Rules
that make or break these: one CTA per email (not two, no "also check out..."), first-person human
voice rather than brand voice, subject lines under 50 characters with no clickbait, every email
delivering something even if the reader never clicks, and no "just checking in" filler. Keep each
email under 150 words.

Input needed: the product, the goal of the sequence, and the audience.

## 1. Sequence strategy

State the goal (activate, convert, retain, reactivate), the trigger event that starts the
sequence, the length in emails and days, and the single metric that says it is working.

## 2. The emails

For each email:

- Internal name and send timing (day N after trigger, or condition-based: "if onboarding
  incomplete").
- Subject line plus one A/B variant, and the inbox preview text.
- Full body copy: an opening line that is personal and relevant (never "hope this finds you
  well"), one core message, one CTA (button text plus where it goes), and an optional P.S. for a
  secondary hook.

Default skeletons to adapt:

Onboarding: welcome + first action (immediately); quick-win tutorial (day 1); core feature (day
3); social proof (day 5); upgrade nudge or "need help?" (day 7); value summary + feedback ask
(day 14).

Re-engagement: "we noticed you haven't..." (day 1 of inactivity); what they missed (day 3);
success story (day 7); the direct ask, what went wrong (day 14); the goodbye email, "we'll stop
emailing" (day 21).

## 3. Technical setup

Recommend a sending tool matched to stage and budget (verify current pricing before citing it),
the user attributes to track for segmentation, unsubscribe handling that satisfies CAN-SPAM/GDPR
(one-click, honored immediately), and the from-name choice (founder name usually beats company
name at this stage).

## 4. Performance targets

Rules of thumb for SaaS lifecycle email, to calibrate rather than quote as fact:

| Metric              | Rough target | Action if below                        |
|---------------------|--------------|----------------------------------------|
| Open rate           | 40%+         | Test subject lines and send time       |
| Click rate          | 8%+          | Simplify the CTA, shorten the email    |
| Unsubscribe rate    | under 0.5%   | Reduce frequency, add value            |
| Sequence completion | 60%+         | Cut or rewrite the weak emails         |
