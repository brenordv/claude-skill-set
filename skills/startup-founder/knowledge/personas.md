# Personas

Generate 3 distinct user personas that read like real people, then rank them so the founder knows
who to build for first.

Input needed: the product and target market. For B2B products, at least one persona is a
technical buyer and one an economic buyer.

## Per-persona template

**1. Identity.** A realistic name (not "Startup Steve"), age, location, job title; company size
and stage for B2B; income or budget range.

**2. Day in the life.** 3-4 sentences of a typical workday: the tools they touch, the meetings
they sit in, the frustrations they hit. It should read like a person, not a market segment.

**3. Goals and motivations.** The primary goal this quarter, the secondary goal they cannot
prioritize, and the underlying motivation (career growth, financial pressure, personal mission).

**4. Pain points.** Exactly 3 frustrations in the problem space. Each must be observable (you
could watch them struggle with it), quotable (written as a sentence they would say in an
interview), and actionable (the product could address it).

**5. Current workarounds.** The tools or processes they use today: what each is used for, what is
broken about it, and roughly how much time or money it wastes per week.

**6. Decision-making.** How they discover new tools, what would make them try this one, what would
make them pay (the specific trigger or pain threshold), and who else is involved in the purchase
for B2B.

**7. Fit score.** Rate 1-5 on urgency (how badly they need this now), willingness to pay (budget
and authority), and reachability (how easy they are to find and market to). Assign an overall
priority: primary, secondary, or tertiary.

## After all three

Produce a priority matrix: one row per persona, columns for urgency, willingness to pay, and
reachability, plus the overall call. Recommend which persona to build for first and defend the
choice in 2-3 sentences.

## Rules

- The three personas must differ in a way that changes what you would build or how you would
  sell; three variations of one person is a failed output.
- Direct quotes are mandatory; they are what makes a persona testable in a real interview.
- Keep each persona under about 400 words.
