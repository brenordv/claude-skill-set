# Go-to-market

A launch plan that works at the $0-$1K/month budget level: pre-launch audience building, launch
day, and the first 90 days. Channel mechanics (best posting times, what gets removed from which
subreddit, Product Hunt rules) shift constantly; verify current platform norms when the runtime
has web access, and present remembered specifics as starting points to confirm.

Input needed: the product, target audience, and launch timeline. Budget assumed under $500/month
unless stated.

## 1. Launch readiness check

Before the plan: is the MVP sufficient to launch (name the minimum feature set), are there enough
reachable users for the channels below to matter (a TAM sanity check), and what single risk is
most likely to sink the launch?

## 2. Pre-launch (2-4 weeks before)

Audience building: where the target audience already gathers (name the specific subreddits,
communities, Slack/Discord groups, and accounts to engage), 3-5 concrete post ideas with their
platforms to build credibility before launch, and a waitlist call (yes or no, and the incentive
if yes).

Asset preparation: the landing page (write it per `landing-page.md`), the demo format (video vs.
GIF vs. live, length, what it shows), and how to gather social proof before there are customers
(beta users, advisors, build-in-public traction).

## 3. Launch day

Pick the 3 platforms that genuinely fit this product, and give per-platform tactics:

- Product Hunt: launch day/time choice, hunter or self-launch, assets to prepare.
- Hacker News: Show HN vs. regular post, title wording, how to handle comments.
- Reddit: which subreddits by name, and the post format that survives moderation there.
- Twitter/X: thread structure and who to tag.
- LinkedIn: post format for B2B and when to post.

Recommending all five is a smell; depth on three beats breadth on five.

## 4. Post-launch growth (first 90 days)

- Channel priority: rank the top 5 channels by expected impact; for each give an estimated CAC
  range (labeled as an estimate), time to results, and the first concrete action this week.
- Content: 5 article or content ideas targeting search intent around the product, each with a
  distribution plan.
- Community and partnerships: 3 communities to engage in, 2 partnership or integration
  candidates, and 1 growth idea specific to this product.

## 5. Launch dashboard

Define the 5 metrics for the first 90 days per `metrics.md`: definition, day-30/60/90 targets,
the measuring tool, and the action when below target.

## 6. Budget allocation

For a $0-$500/month budget: the split across channels, what stays free vs. what is worth paying
for, and one investment under $200 with outsized expected return.
